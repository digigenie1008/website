Still have a question?
What is the minimum age to start? +

We typically start from age 6 for instruments like Piano and Keyboard. For younger kids, we assess their interest and readiness in a relaxed introductory session.

Do you provide certificates? +

Yes! We prepare students for Trinity or Royal Schools of Music certifications upon request. Many of our students have passed with distinction.

How many classes per week? +

Standard plans include 1 or 2 sessions per week. Each session lasts approximately 45–60 minutes, depending on the student's level.

Can I do online classes? +

Absolutely. Our online students learn just as effectively as in-studio students. All you need is a decent internet connection and your instrument.
